Senior Biostatistician II
Northline Clinical Research - Raleigh, NC (Hybrid, 3 days on-site)
Full-time

About the role
Northline Clinical Research partners with mid-size pharmaceutical sponsors to
run Phase II and Phase III oncology trials. We are seeking a Senior
Biostatistician II to act as lead statistician on two ongoing Phase III
studies, owning the statistical analysis plan from protocol design through
regulatory submission.

Responsibilities
- Author and maintain statistical analysis plans (SAPs) for Phase II/III trials
- Serve as lead statistician on sponsor calls and at FDA advisory meetings
- Produce and validate CDISC-compliant ADaM datasets and TLF packages
- Mentor junior biostatisticians and review their deliverables
- Represent biostatistics on cross-functional study teams

Required qualifications
- PhD in Biostatistics or Statistics
- Minimum 6 years of biostatistics experience in a CRO or pharmaceutical setting
- Expert-level SAS programming, including macro development
- Deep knowledge of survival analysis, mixed models, and group sequential designs
- Demonstrated experience supporting regulatory submissions (NDA/BLA)

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with R and with Bayesian adaptive trial designs
- Familiarity with CDISC SDTM and ADaM standards
- Prior experience in oncology therapeutic areas

Compensation and benefits
Base salary range: $145,000 - $175,000 per year, depending on experience.
Annual bonus target of 12%, 401(k) match, and 25 days of paid time off.

Work authorization
Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the United States. Northline
Clinical Research is unable to provide visa sponsorship for this position at
this time.
